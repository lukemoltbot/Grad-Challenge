# Executive Summary — 2026 Graduate Challenge

## Wallaby Mining: Strategic Assessment & Post-Mining Liability Review

---

### The Situation

Wallaby Mining (WM) acquired 60% of the Waratah Resources Joint Venture, gaining three assets in Queensland's Bowen Basin:

| Asset | Status |
|-------|--------|
| **Springbok Coal Complex** | Operational underground coking coal mine — ceases production Q4 2031 |
| **Brumby Open Cut** | Historic depleted mine + undeveloped Xanadu seam target (25 km NE) |
| **Brave Blossom Project** | Concept-stage underground mine — ~6 Mtpa saleable hard coking coal, ~20-year mine life (2033–2052) |

The acquisition was conditional on **assuming a $900 million closure liability** from 2032 onwards — the cost of shutting down, rehabilitating, and maintaining the Springbok site for 20 years post-closure. With Springbok closing in 2031 and no replacement revenue lined up, the company faces a stark choice: **pay $900M with zero income, or invest in a new mine that defers that bill and generates 20 years of revenue.**

---

### The Two Core Tasks

| Task | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Task 1 — Strategic Review** | Evaluate all life-extension options (Brave Blossom, Brumby, Bronco acquisition, future exploration, or no-go). SWOT across 11 areas for each. Recommend go/no-go with forward work plan. |
| **Task 2 — Closure Liability** | Evaluate the $900M closure estimate and post-mining carbon liability. Present SMART-aligned recommendations to reduce both. All targets implementable by end of 2027. |

---

### The Five Options Evaluated

| Option | Summary | Verdict |
|--------|---------|---------|
| **A: Brave Blossom Full Go** | Build the new underground mine — $389M capital, 20-year life | ✅ Recommended — but via staged approach (Option C) |
| **B: Do Nothing** | Close Springbok, pay $900M, walk away | ❌ Rejected — financially destructive |
| **C: Phased Go** | Same as A, but with 3 checkpoint gates before major capital | ✅ **Primary recommendation** |
| **D: Buy Bronco's Lease** | Acquire neighbouring mine (ML1654) | ⚠️ Strategic backup — Bronco has rejected all offers |
| **E: Future Exploration** | $5–10M regional exploration drilling | ⚠️ Parallel workstream only — revenue 20+ years away |

**Key logic**: Options B and E both leave the $900M closure bill due with no revenue — not a strategy, but a slow-motion financial crisis. Option D can't be evaluated (no resource data, seller won't engage). Option C dominates Option A: same upside, dramatically lower risk.

---

### The Three Recommendations

#### Recommendation 1: Stage-Gated Brave Blossom Development

Commit **$20M initially** for a Pre-Feasibility Study and exploration drilling (2027–2028). Three formal gates control all subsequent capital:

| Gate | Timing | Decision | If No-Go |
|------|--------|----------|----------|
| **Gate 1** | End 2028 | PFS results positive? Federal approval pathway clear? | $20M sunk — stop or pursue alternatives |
| **Gate 2** | End 2030 | Approvals secured? Rail/port capacity confirmed? CHPP life extension committed? | $167.5M sunk — fall back to 75%-within-ML plan (~15-year life, still $748M NPV) |
| **Gate 3** | 2032 | Longwall delivered? First coal achieved? | Committed — confirmation gate only |

**Capital phasing**: $20M (Stage 1) → $104M (Stage 2) → $265M (Stage 3) = $389M total, gated at each stage.

#### Recommendation 2: Optimise the $900M Closure Liability

The $900M is a third-party estimate built on challengeable assumptions. The audit identified 6 specific savings:

| # | SMART Target | Saving |
|---|-------------|--------|
| 1 | Remove duplicate TSF costing (Domain 2) | $39.5–43.8M |
| 2 | Reduce contingency 35%→25% | $49.4M |
| 3 | Sell 505 company houses instead of demolishing | $11.8M |
| 4 | Accelerate progressive rehabilitation ($2.5M→$5M/yr) | $11.2M |
| 5 | Progressive lease relinquishment | ~$50M |
| 6 | Monetise gas drainage post-closure | $14.1M cost→revenue |
| | **Total** | **~$180.3M (20%)** |

#### Recommendation 3: Carbon Abatement & Gas Monetisation

Keep existing gas drainage infrastructure running post-closure to capture methane, sell gas, and generate carbon credits:

- **4.68M tCO₂e** decommissioned mine emissions over 20 years — unmanaged, this is a ~$700M liability at mid-range carbon prices
- Gas capture abates **70%** of emissions
- Converts a $14.1M cost into a revenue stream
- Infrastructure already exists — extension is a modification, not a new build

### The Financial Case

#### Critical Correction

The original workbook computed Brave Blossom NPV at **$1,320M** — but the Capital tab was **empty**, meaning the mine was modelled as if it cost $0 to build. After populating the 12 capital items with real year-by-year scheduling and a 30% contingency:

| Metric | Original (Workbook) | Corrected |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| Brave Blossom NPV | $1,320M | **$997M** |
| Combined NPV (Springbok + Brave Blossom) | $1,670M | **$1,277M** |
| Brave Blossom IRR | 77.4% | **~52%** |
| Scenario range | — | **$859M–$1,072M** (all positive) |

All five scenarios remain strongly NPV-positive even after the correction. The worst case (full contingency, no tax shield) is still $859M.

#### The Deferral Lever

The most powerful argument isn't the NPV — it's the **capital-to-liability ratio**:

> **Every $1 invested in Brave Blossom defers $2.31 of closure liability.**

Even if the mine produced zero coal, the deferral alone generates ~**$185M net positive NPV** ($508M deferral value at 7% discount, minus $323M present value of capital at 8%). Revenue from 20 years of coking coal production builds on top of that base.

### The Bottom Line

> **GO — but a gated Go.**

1. Authorise **$20M for Stage 1** (PFS + drilling + approvals + closure review + carbon study) in 2027
2. Commence **closure liability optimisation** immediately — target $180M reduction by end-2027
3. Commence **carbon abatement planning** immediately — gas capture, ACCU generation, Safeguard compliance
4. Maintain **Bronco as strategic optionality** — monitor, prepare, don't commit
5. Run **Brumby PFS and regional exploration** as parallel workstreams ($10–18M combined)
6. **Defer major capital decisions** to Gate 1 (end 2028), Gate 2 (end 2030), Gate 3 (2032)

This approach commits minimal capital now, funds the studies that de-risk the concept, preserves no-go optionality at every gate, defers $2.31 of closure liability per $1 invested, and — if gates pass — delivers a 20-year revenue extension on a $997M NPV base while retaining 800+ jobs and keeping Dustyroo Flats alive.
