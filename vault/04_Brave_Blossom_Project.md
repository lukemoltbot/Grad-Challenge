# 04 — Brave Blossom Project (Conceptual)

## Overview

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Location | Immediately NE of Springbok within the Springbok Coal Complex |
| Type | Underground longwall — re-entry of sealed historic workings |
| Production Target | ~6mtpa saleable hard coking coal (8mtpa ROM) |
| Mine Life | ~20 years (to ~2052) |
| Longwall Width | 300m (walk-on/walk-off) |
| Status | Concept stage — limited drilling completed, further resource definition needed |
| Old Workings | Sealed in early 2000s after persistent roof falls. Portal seals in good shape. Water ingress unknown. |

## Geology & Geotechnical

### Stratigraphy
- **Mentelle Formation** (sub-unit of Blackwater Group)
- Overlies barren Ingelara Formation; underlies Fairhill Formation
- Two economically extractable seams: **Mentelle 1 & 5**
- **Mentelle 5 seam** is the primary target

### Structural Features
- Conditions expected similar to Springbok (normal & reverse faults, monoclines, dykes)
- **Major monocline** over mine plan — localised seam grades up to 1 in [value truncated in PDF]
- **Dome-like structure** over eastern portion
- Two dominant fault sets: NNW-trending and NE-trending
- Historic bolt-up plans show high-density bolting + mega bolts

### Working Section
- Mentelle 5 thins as mine plan progresses East → **more dilution in feed**

### Coal Quality
- Expected deterioration vs Springbok's Premium HCC
- Similar yield to current operations
- Long-term customers anticipated to accept at **12% discount** to current contracted price

## Ventilation & Gas

- Pre/post-drainage alone **insufficient** — new ventilation network required before operations
- New intake/exhaust shafts and fans needed (located away from open cut dumps)
- Gas content: 12-15m³/t at 300m depth, increasing with depth
- Methane ratio (CH4/(CH4+CO2)): 0.98 average
- Overlying seam gas content: **unknown**
- Spontaneous combustion: relatively low propensity (but coal roof in goaf poses risk)
- Gas drainage required for outburst mitigation + high productivity longwall support
- Methane content falls within energy company contract specs — clauses allow extension

## Equipment & Infrastructure

- **No residual capacity** expected in Springbok equipment at LOM end
- Limited pre-existing infrastructure at Springbok (beyond CHPP) can service Brave Blossom
- Full equipment list required: longwall, mining equipment, conveyors, ventilation, drift, ROM bin, stockpile
- Management must prepare capital estimate in "Brave Blossom Capital" tab of valuation spreadsheet

## Workforce

- Long-term manning: ~800 staff and contractors
- Springbok workforce suitably skilled to transition
- Significant proportion currently contracted labour
- Owner operator vs contract miner decision required

## Environment & Community

- Minimal additional environmental limitations from underground workings
- Community will appreciate extended mine life and support
- Closure liability deferred until after Brave Blossom completion

## Mine Closure (Brave Blossom)

- Incremental rehabilitation cost: **$2.5M AUD** (pit void + infrastructure)
- Does not factor in legacy rehabilitation ($900M)
- Liability deferred to later years following Brave Blossom completion
