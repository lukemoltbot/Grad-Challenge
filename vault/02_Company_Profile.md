## Asset Portfolio

| Asset | Type | Status | Notes |
|-------|------|--------|-------|
| **Springbok Coal Complex** | Underground mine | Operational | 5 years from closure (~2031). Premium hard coking coal. 1× longwall, 3× development miners. ~3.5mtpa historic production. |
| **Brumby Open Cut** | Historic open cut | Ceased | Produced ~70/30 thermal-to-coking split. Depleted within ML. Resource extends beyond ML boundary. |
| **Brave Blossom Project** | Underground project | Concept stage | ~6mtpa saleable HCC until 2052. Re-entry of sealed workings. $400M+ capital required. |

## Waratah Resources Joint Venture (JV)

- WM holds 60% interest
- JV partners include Japanese customers (who also buy the coal)
- The Brave Blossom MDL (~25% of mine plan) is owned by the JV
- Japanese customers are members of the JV

## Location

- Springbok Coal Complex: ~80km NW of Emerald, Queensland's Bowen Basin
- Barada People hold registered native title over the land
- Local town: Dustyroo Flats (economy nearly entirely dependent on the complex)

## Neighbouring Operations

- **Bronco Mining** — shares northern lease boundary (ML1654)
  - Small open cut, currently operating
  - WM has approached Bronco board multiple times to purchase — **rejected each time**
  - Bronco has negotiated a royalty-based extraction deal with a neighbouring mine for resource within their lease
