## Current Challenges

### Geotechnical & Gas
- Underground operations progressing deeper — **gassier and more geotechnically challenging**
- Worsening geotechnical conditions and gas drainage impacting development schedule
- **Longwall discontinuity** — park up in 2027, followed by minimal longwall float for remaining LOM

### Gas Contract
- Agreement with local energy producer to send gas offsite for power generation
- Contract expires at **last coal** unless clauses triggered prior
- Methane from Brave Blossom falls within contract specs (extension possible if project proceeds)

### CHPP (Coal Handling & Preparation Plant)
- Design capacity: 8Mt/year feed
- Produces ~4mtpa saleable @ ~67% yield (Premium Hard Coking Coal)
- Conveyed from underground to CHPP
- Rejects hauled to pit void; tailings pumped to above-ground TSF adjacent to CHPP
- **End of life: 2032** at current throughput
- Extension requires: new conveyor, raw coal bin overhauls, significant structural repairs

### Rail & Port
- Current contracts (2025): 4mtpa through to 2028
- Underperformance has created **carried take-or-pay (TOP) liability** — DSE cost +$10/saleable tonne
- 2028-2031 negotiations underway — costs expected in line, BUT 2× large new competing mines will remove residual network capacity from 2028
- Renewal request must be submitted **1 year prior to expiry**

## Social Context

- WM is the major supporter of local government and town (Dustyroo Flats)
- Town economy nearly entirely dependent on the complex
- Competing mining company faced media backlash for closing a mine and discontinuing town support
- Procurement struggling — local suppliers diversifying away from mining, increased lead times
