# 15 — Brumby Open Cut (Development Project)

> **Source**: 8-page supplemental PDF (page 3). This file reconciles the 8-page PDF's description of Brumby as a **development target** with the 18-page brief's description of Brumby as a **historic ceased mine** (vault file 02).

## Dual Status

The Brumby open cut has two distinct phases:

1. **Historic operation** (captured in vault file 02): Previously produced a 70/30 thermal-to-coking split. Reserves depleted within the existing ML. Resource extends beyond ML boundary into neighbouring lease.
2. **Development project** (new from 8-page PDF): A proposed new open cut targeting the Xanadu seam in an adjacent tenement.

## Strategic Significance

- **Brumby is the "historic open cut mine"** acquired in the 2016 JV deal. The 8-page PDF clarifies: WM acquired "1 operational underground, 1 historic open cut mine and 1 coal development project" — Brumby is the historic open cut, Brave Blossom is the coal development project.
- The Xanadu seam development represents a **potential third life extension option** alongside Brave Blossom and the "do nothing" scenario.
- Low strip ratio + open cut = potentially lower capital and faster ramp than Brave Blossom underground.
- Could bridge cashflow gap between Springbok closure (2031) and Brave Blossom first coal (~2033).
- The 70/30 thermal/HCC split means revenue depends on thermal coal market, which is more volatile than coking coal.
- The resource extends beyond the ML into Bronco Mining's lease (ML1654) — any development may require land/access negotiation with Bronco.

## Relationship to Existing Vault

- Vault file 02 lists Brumby as "Historic, Ceased, Depleted within ML" — this is the **historic status**.
- Vault file 05 mentions "Resource extends beyond Springbok ML into Bronco's lease at similar strip ratio & quality" — this supports the development case.
- Vault file 08 (Option D: Bronco Acquisition) considers acquiring Bronco's lease for resource extension — Brumby Xanadu seam development is the alternative to acquisition.
- The 8-page PDF presents Brumby as a **viable development project** with defined targets, not just a depleted asset.
