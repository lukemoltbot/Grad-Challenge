# 09 — Task 2: Closure Liability & Post-Mining Mitigation

## Objective

Evaluate current Springbok closure estimate and post-mining liability. Present recommendations to minimise post-mining liability, including underground decommissioned mine emissions.

### Carbon/Decommissioned Mine Emissions
- Emissions continue for **20 years** post-cessation (NGER Determination)
- Sources: fossil fuel combustion, post-mining activities, coal mine waste gas, decommissioned mine emissions
- Safeguard Mechanism: >100,000 tonnes CO₂-e/yr triggers regulation
- Emissions above baseline → abatement or carbon credit purchase
- Baseline levels outlined in valuation model
- Gas assignment model covers current operations + proposed project site

## Mitigation Strategies to Evaluate

### 1. Rehabilitation Cost Reduction
- Review third-party estimate methodology (QLD ERC tool, unit rates, activity-based costing)
- Challenge unit rates vs market rates
- Identify opportunities for progressive rehabilitation acceleration (do more now while revenue flows)
- Pit void utilisation — can voids be repurposed (water storage, tailings, solar, etc.)?
- Negotiate with third party for revised scope/rates

### 2. Decommissioned Mine Emissions Reduction
- **Gas drainage continuation** — capture methane post-closure for power generation or flaring
- **Seal integrity** — ensure portal seals and vent shaft seals prevent fugitive emissions
- **Backfill / flooding** — flood sealed workings to suppress methane release
- **Reforestation/vegetation** — bio-sequestration on rehabilitated land
- **Carbon credits** — generate ACCUs (Australian Carbon Credit Units) from:
  - Captured methane destruction
  - Reforestation projects
  - Soil carbon improvement on rehabilitated land
- **Safeguard Mechanism compliance** — model baseline, forecast emissions, plan abatement pathway

### 3. Closure Liability Deferral (via Brave Blossom)
- If Brave Blossom proceeds, $900M liability deferred to post-2052
- 20+ years of additional revenue to fund closure
- Workforce retained, closure expertise maintained
- Progressive rehabilitation can continue during extended mine life

### 4. Gas Contract Extension
- Current gas offsite agreement expires at last coal (unless clauses triggered)
- Methane from Brave Blossom within contract specs
- Extending gas capture beyond closure → revenue + emission reduction
- Potential to negotiate favourable terms as energy transition accelerates

### 5. Social License & Community Transition
- Plan workforce transition early — avoid shock closure
- Skills transfer programs (mining → rehabilitation/closure roles)
- Explore alternative industries for Dustyroo Flats (renewables, agriculture, tourism)
- Engage Barada People in closure planning and post-mining land use

## SMART Criteria Requirements

All recommendations must be:
- **S**pecific — clearly defined action
- **M**easurable — quantified target (e.g., "$X reduction in liability")
- **A**chievable — realistic given constraints
- **R**elevant — addresses the board's objectives
- **T**ime-bound — clear timeline for delivery

## Deliverable for Task 2

Clear recommendations on post-mining liability mitigation, presented in SMART format, covering:
- Rehabilitation cost reduction
- Carbon liability management
- Decommissioned mine emission abatement
- Community transition planning
