## 1. THE SCENARIO

You are the **management team of Wallaby Mining (WM)**, presenting to the WM Board of Directors. WM acquired **60% of the Waratah Resources JV**, gaining three assets:

| Asset | Status |
|-------|--------|
| **Springbok Coal Complex** | Operational underground coking coal mine, ~5 years from closure (Q4 2031) |
| **Brumby Open Cut** | Historic depleted mine + **development project** targeting Xanadu seam (1.5Mtpa, 25km NE, 70/30 GCN/PHCC) |
| **Brave Blossom Project** | Concept-stage underground, ~6mtpa saleable HCC until ~2052, ~$389M capital |

The acquisition was **conditional on assuming closure liability from 2032 onwards**. In return, WM receives 60% of revenue from pre-existing infrastructure (CHPP, TLO, rail/port contracts).

## 2. THE TWO CORE TASKS

### TASK 1 — Strategic Review of Life Extension Options

Evaluate all options for extending mine life beyond Springbok's 2031 cessation:

| Option | Description | Capital | Revenue Extension | Closure Impact |
|--------|-------------|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| **A: Brave Blossom Full Go** | Proceed with Brave Blossom UG | ~$389M | 2033–2052 (20 yrs) | $900M deferred to post-2052 |
| **B: No-Go** | Mine Springbok to closure, execute rehabilitation | $0 | None | $900M due 2031–2050 |
| **C: Phased Go** | Commence studies 2027, defer capital to PFS outcome | $20M (studies) | Conditional | Conditional deferral |
| **D: Bronco Acquisition** | Acquire/royalty deal with Bronco for resource extension | Unknown | Unknown | Partial deferral |
| **E: Future Projects** | Defer Brave Blossom, assess new exploration targets | $5–10M | Post-2052 | $900M due 2031–2050 |

**Key constraint**: If Brave Blossom proceeds, forward work plan must commence **2027** to ensure cashflow continuity beyond 2030 (Springbok cessation). The board wants a **go/no-go** recommendation.

### TASK 2 — Closure Liability & Post-Mining Mitigation

Evaluate the **$900M** Springbok closure estimate (significantly higher than anticipated) and present recommendations to minimise post-mining liability:

- **Rehabilitation liability**: $900M (third-party estimate using QLD ERC tool)
  - $494M direct works + $173M contingency + $233M holding costs (20-year period)
  - 9 domains, 284 line items, 296 unit rates — fully audited ✅
- **Carbon/decommissioned mine emissions**: Continue for 20 years post-cessation (NGER Determination)
  - Safeguard Mechanism: >100,000 t CO₂-e/yr triggers regulation
  - Springbok: 1.6M tCO₂e above baseline (5 years)
  - Brave Blossom: 20.1M tCO₂e above baseline (20 years)
  - Decommissioned mine: 4.68M tCO₂e over ~20 years (combined scenario)
- **All recommendations must follow SMART criteria** (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

**Target timeline**: Recommendations must be implementable by **end of calendar year 2027**.

## 3. DELIVERABLES — THE 15-MINUTE BOARD PRESENTATION

### Format
- **15 minutes** presentation (maximum)
- **5 minutes** Q&A from the board
- Professional, creative, influential — this is a **board pitch, not a technical report**
- Team decides who presents and who fields Q&A

### Presentation Structure — 4 Required Sections

#### Section 1: Brave Blossom UG — Valuation & SWOT
- [ ] **Valuation analysis** (NPV/IRR) using the provided financial model
  - Model shows: Brave Blossom NPV = **$1,320M** (standalone, 8% discount)
  - Combined (Springbok + Brave Blossom) NPV = **$1,670M**
  - Springbok standalone NPV = **$279M**
- [ ] **SWOT analysis** covering all 11 areas (see Section 4 below)
- [ ] **Capital estimate completed** in "Brave Blossom Capital" tab of valuation spreadsheet (currently a template shell — **must be populated**)

#### Section 2: Current Operations — Post-Mining Liability Reduction
- [ ] **Formal review** of end-of-life liabilities (rehabilitation + carbon)
- [ ] **SMART-aligned recommendations** to reduce rehabilitation and/or carbon liabilities
  - 6 quantified SMART targets already identified (see Section 5) — ~$162M (18%) reduction
- [ ] **Resultant cashflow analysis & NPV impact** of reductions
- [ ] **Implementation risk / achievability** assessment
- [ ] **Improvement opportunities** identified and evaluated by site team
- [ ] Target timeline: **end of 2027**

#### Section 3: Other Proposed Projects — Forward Workplan & SWOT
- [ ] **Strategic review** of other prospective projects (Brumby open cut, Bronco acquisition, future targets)

- [ ] **Concept valuation** completed (blank valuation spreadsheet available for this)

- [ ] All forward work programs have **accompanying business case** (financial model)

#### Section 4: Recommendation — Go/No-Go & Timeline
- [ ] **Consider the "do nothing" option** (no extension beyond current mine life)
- [ ] **Recommendation** on whether to proceed with forward work plan
- [ ] **Proposed timeline** for progression
- [ ] Recommendations reflect **early-stage nature** and broader strategic fit
- [ ] **Top 3 recommendations**, each with:
  - Resultant cashflow analysis
  - Implementation risks

## 4. SWOT ANALYSIS — 11 REQUIRED AREAS (per option)

Each option's SWOT must address ALL of these:

1. Mine plan physicals & coal quality considerations
2. Infrastructure requirements
3. Equipment suitability, adequacy, availability & utilisation
4. Geological / geotechnical risk
5. Approvals / environmental risk
6. Carbon liability
7. Deliverability / complexity of the plan
8. Financial implications
9. Labour supply (owner operator vs contract miner)
10. People impact / change management (work process, roster patterns, housing, health & safety, training)
11. Any other considerations for the board

## 5. SMART CRITERIA (Task 2 Recommendations)

All closure liability recommendations must be:
- **Specific** — What exactly will be done?
- **Measurable** — How will success be quantified? (e.g., "$X reduction")
- **Achievable** — Is it realistic given resources/constraints?
- **Relevant** — Does it address the board's objective?
- **Time-bound** — When will it be delivered?

| 6 | Monetise gas drainage post-closure | $14.1M cost→revenue | 1.6%+ | Q2 2032 |
