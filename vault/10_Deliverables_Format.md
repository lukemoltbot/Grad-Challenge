# 10 — Deliverables & Presentation Format

## Presentation Structure

| Section | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Duration | 15 minutes (maximum) |
| Q&A | 5 minutes from the board |
| Format | Professional, creative, influential |
| Presenters | Team decides — can be one person or multiple |
| Q&A responders | Team decides who fields board questions |

## Required Content

### Top 3 Recommendations
Each recommendation must include:
1. **Resultant cashflow analysis** — financial impact modelled
2. **Implementation risks** — what could go wrong, mitigation measures

### Strategic Direction Recommendations
- **Valuation analysis** — NPV/IRR or comparable metrics for each option
- **SWOT analysis** — strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats

### Forward Work Plan
- Whether to progress with a forward work plan on Brave Blossom
- When this will occur (timing)
- Recommended future work on additional potential projects

## SWOT Analysis Topics

Each option's SWOT must address:
1. Mine plan physicals & coal quality considerations
2. Infrastructure requirements
3. Equipment suitability, adequacy, and implications for availability & utilisation
4. Geological / geotechnical risk
5. Approvals / environmental risk
6. Carbon liability
7. Deliverability / complexity of the plan
8. Financial implications of the options
9. Labour supply considerations (owner operator vs contract miner)
10. People impact including change management:
    - Work process changes
    - Roster patterns
    - Housing requirements
    - Health & safety
    - Training requirements
11. Any other considerations for the board

## SMART Criteria (Task 2 Recommendations)

All closure liability recommendations must follow:
- **Specific** — What exactly will be done?
- **Measurable** — How will success be quantified?
- **Achievable** — Is it realistic given resources/constraints?
- **Relevant** — Does it address the board's objective?
- **Time-bound** — When will it be delivered?

## Financial Model Requirements

- Financial model provided for both current underground operation and the project
- Must consider key capital requirements to support any project
- Must prepare the capital estimate (in "Brave Blossom Capital" tab of valuation spreadsheet)
- All forward work programs must have accompanying business case (financial model)

## Tips for a Strong Submission

1. **Lead with the recommendation** — board wants a clear answer, not a cliffhanger
2. **Quantify everything** — use the financial model to show NPV, payback, IRR
3. **Address the "no-go" scenario** — show you've considered not proceeding
4. **Acknowledge risks honestly** — don't hide the federal approval uncertainty
5. **Present a timeline** — when does each action start, what are the milestones?
6. **Be creative** — the brief explicitly asks for creativity
7. **Influence** — this is a board pitch, not a technical report
