## Washability Data (Figure 3 — Ash-Yield Curves)

Wet-tumbled in-seam samples analysed at six separation densities (F1.35–F1.8):

**Yield vs Feed Ash**: Below ~15% feed ash, yields range 75% (F1.35) to ~89% (F1.8). Above 15%, yield drops sharply — F1.35 reaches ~0% at 70% feed ash, F1.8 maintains ~48%.

**Product Ash vs Feed Ash**: Below ~15% feed ash, product ash ranges 7.5% (F1.35) to ~10% (F1.8). At 70% feed ash: F1.35 ~16%, F1.8 ~27%.

**Key insight**: Coal is highly washable up to ~15% feed ash. Working section thins eastward → more dilution → higher feed ash → yield/quality trade-off becomes critical.
