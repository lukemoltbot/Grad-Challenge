# 01 — Challenge Overview

## What Is This Challenge?

The 2026 Graduate Challenge is a **business strategy simulation** set in the Australian coal mining industry. You act as the **management team of Wallaby Mining (WM)**, presenting to the WM Board of Directors.

## The Scenario

WM acquired 60% of the Waratah Resources Joint Venture (JV), gaining:
- One operational underground coking coal mine (Springbok)
- One historic open cut (Brumby — depleted within ML)
- One underground project at concept stage (Brave Blossom)

The acquisition was conditional on **assuming closure liability from 2032 onwards**. In return, WM receives 60% of revenue from pre-existing infrastructure.

## Two Core Tasks

### Task 1: Strategic Assessment of Life Extension Options
- Review the Brave Blossom Underground project and any future prospective projects/extensions
- Consider the option of **not extending** beyond current mine life
- Intent is **strategic** (Brave Blossom is at concept stage)
- Present SWOT analysis for each option
- Recommend whether to progress a forward work plan and when

### Task 2: Closure Estimate & Post-Mining Liability
- Evaluate current Springbok closure estimate ($900M — significantly higher than anticipated)
- Present recommendations to **minimise post-mining liability**, including:
  - Underground decommissioned mine emissions
  - Carbon liability under Safeguard Mechanism
- Recommendations must follow **SMART criteria** (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)

## Key Constraint

The board wants a **go/no-go** recommendation on Brave Blossom plus a forward work plan timeline. If Brave Blossom proceeds, the work plan must commence in **2027** to ensure cashflow continuity beyond 2030 (Springbok cessation).

## Deliverable Format

- **15 minutes** presentation (max)
- **5 minutes** Q&A from the board
- Professional, creative, influential
- Team decides presenters and Q&A responders
