## Summary

Wallaby Mining (WM) acquired 60% of the Waratah Resources JV, gaining:
- **Springbok Coal Complex** — operational underground coking coal mine, 5 years from closure
- **Brumby Open Cut** — historic mine (depleted within ML) + development project targeting Xanadu seam (1.5Mtpa, 25km NE)
- **Brave Blossom Project** — underground concept, ~6mtpa saleable HCC until 2052

**Two core tasks:**
1. **Strategic review** of life extension options (Brave Blossom + future projects vs. no extension)
2. **Closure liability mitigation** — reduce the $900M rehabilitation estimate and post-mining emissions

**Deliverable**: 15-min presentation + 5-min Q&A to the board with top 3 recommendations, cashflow analysis, SWOT, and forward work plan.
