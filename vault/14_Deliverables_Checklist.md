# 14 — Master Deliverables Checklist

> Consolidated from both the 18-page brief (vault source) and the 8-page supplemental PDF (by Shaun Booth, 30 June 2026). Deduplicated against files 08, 09, 10.

## Presentation Format

- [ ] **15-minute presentation** (maximum)
- [ ] **5-minute Q&A** with the board
- [ ] Professional, creative, influential
- [ ] Team decides who presents and who fields Q&A

## Presentation Must Cover (4 Sections)

### Section 1: Brave Blossom UG — Valuation & SWOT
- [ ] Valuation analysis (NPV/IRR) using provided financial model
- [ ] SWOT analysis covering all 11 areas (see file 13)
- [ ] Capital estimate completed in "Brave Blossom Capital" tab of valuation spreadsheet
- [ ] Key capital requirements identified and reviewed

### Section 2: Current Operations — Post-Mining Liability Reduction
- [ ] Formal review of end-of-life liabilities (rehabilitation + carbon)
- [ ] SMART-aligned recommendations to reduce rehabilitation and/or carbon liabilities
- [ ] Resultant cashflow analysis & NPV impact
- [ ] Implementation risk/achievability assessment
- [ ] Improvement opportunities identified and evaluated by site team
- [ ] Target timeline: end of calendar year 2027

### Section 3: Other Proposed Projects — Forward Workplan & SWOT
- [ ] Strategic review of any other prospective projects (including Brumby open cut)
- [ ] SWOT analysis for each project/opportunity
- [ ] Concept valuation completed (blank valuation spreadsheet available for this)
- [ ] Forward workplan addressing key risks and next steps
- [ ] All forward work programs have accompanying business case (financial model)

### Section 4: Recommendation — Go/No-Go & Timeline
- [ ] Consider the "do nothing" option (no extension beyond current mine life)
- [ ] Recommendation on whether to proceed with forward work plan
- [ ] Proposed timeline for progression
- [ ] Recommendations reflect early-stage nature and broader strategic fit

## SWOT Analysis — 11 Required Areas (per option)

1. Mine plan physicals & coal quality
2. Infrastructure requirements
3. Equipment suitability, adequacy, availability & utilisation
4. Geological / geotechnical risk
5. Approvals / environmental risk
6. Carbon liability
7. Deliverability / complexity
8. Financial implications
9. Labour supply (owner operator vs contract miner)
10. People impact / change management (work process, roster, housing, H&S, training)
11. Any other considerations for the board

## SMART Criteria (Task 2 Recommendations)

All closure liability recommendations must be:
- [ ] **Specific** — what exactly will be done
- [ ] **Measurable** — how success is quantified
- [ ] **Achievable** — realistic given constraints
- [ ] **Relevant** — addresses board's objective
- [ ] **Time-bound** — when delivered

## Financial Model Requirements

- [ ] Financial model for current underground operation (provided)
- [ ] Financial model for Brave Blossom project (provided)
- [ ] Capital estimate completed in "Brave Blossom Capital" tab
- [ ] Blank valuation available for other potential project options
- [ ] Revised forward pricing curves from marketing team (in model)
- [ ] Emission estimates and baseline levels (in model)

## Key Reminders

1. Lead with the recommendation — board wants a clear answer
2. Quantify everything — NPV, payback, IRR
3. Address the "no-go" scenario explicitly
4. Acknowledge risks honestly (especially federal approval uncertainty)
5. Present a timeline with milestones
6. Be creative — the brief explicitly asks for it
7. This is a board pitch, not a technical report
