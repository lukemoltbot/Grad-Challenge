# 08 — Task 1: Strategic Review of Life Extension Options

## Objective

Conduct a strategic review of life extension options within the Springbok Coal Complex:
1. **Brave Blossom Underground** — concept-stage project
2. **Future prospective projects** — targets suggested by historic mining, not yet assessed
3. **No extension** — manage Springbok through to closure (baseline scenario)

The intent is **strategic** (not detailed engineering). Recommendations should be made with this in mind.

## Options to Evaluate

### Option A: Progress Brave Blossom (Go)
- Re-enter sealed workings, establish new longwall operation
- ~6mtpa saleable HCC until ~2052
- Forward work plan must commence 2027 for cashflow continuity beyond 2030
- Capital: ~$389M (concept estimate)
- Revenue at 12% discount to current price, 60% share to WM
- Defers $900M closure liability to post-2052

### Option B: No Life Extension (No-Go)
- Mine Springbok through to Q4 2031 cessation
- Execute closure from 2031, target relinquishment by 2050
- Pay $900M rehabilitation liability (potentially optimised)
- Carbon liability for decommissioned mine emissions (20 years post-cessation)
- Town impact — Dustyroo Flats economic collapse
- Loss of workforce to other operations
- CHPP, rail/port contracts lapse

### Option C: Delayed/Phased Approach
- Commence studies in 2027 but defer capital commitment
- De-risk through PFS before committing $389M
- Risk: workforce dispersion, cost escalation, approval delays
- Potential to monetise gas drainage during study phase

### Option D: Acquire Bronco Mining Open Cut
- Resource extends beyond Springbok ML into Bronco's lease
- Bronco rejected previous approaches, but circumstances may change
- Royalty-based extraction model (like Bronco's deal with neighbour) as alternative
- Smaller scale, open cut — potentially lower capital, faster start
- Could bridge cashflow gap if Brave Blossom is delayed

### Option E: Future Prospective Projects (Beyond Brave Blossom)
- Historic mining suggests further targets
- Not yet assessed — require concept studies
- Could extend life beyond 2052
- Long lead times for studies, approvals, infrastructure

## SWOT Requirements

Each option must include SWOT analysis covering:
- Mine plan physicals & coal quality considerations
- Infrastructure requirements
- Equipment suitability, adequacy, availability & utilisation
- Geological / geotechnical risk
- Approvals / environmental risk
- Carbon liability
- Deliverability / complexity of the plan
- Financial implications
- Labour supply (owner operator vs contract miner)
- People impact / change management (work process, roster patterns, housing, health & safety, training)
- Any other considerations for the board

## Deliverable for Task 1

- Top 3 recommendations with:
  - Resultant cashflow analysis
  - Implementation risks
- Strategic direction recommendations with:
  - Valuation and SWOT analysis
- Recommendation on whether to progress forward work plan and when
- Recommended future work on additional potential projects
