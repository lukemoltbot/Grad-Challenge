## Part B — SMART Recommendations (Quantified from Workbook)

### SMART-1: Challenge the Duplicate TSF Costing

- **S (Specific)**: Engage independent QS to verify Domain 2 item 2.002 — two rates ($39.5M + $43.8M) exist for the same line item with identical descriptions and comments
- **M (Measurable)**: Remove duplicate — target **$39.5M–$43.8M reduction** in Domain 2
- **A (Achievable)**: Line item is clearly duplicated (same description, same comment, same item number) — straightforward to verify
- **R (Relevant)**: Directly reduces closure liability; 4.4–4.9% of $900M
- **T (Time-bound)**: Complete verification by Q2 2027, revised ERC by Q3 2027

---

### SMART-2: Reduce Contingency from 35% to 25%

- **S**: Negotiate reduced contingency rates — PM at 5% (not 10%), monitoring at 5% (not 10%), general at 15% (unchanged)
- **M**: Saving = 10% × $494M = **$49.4M reduction** (with flow-on to holding, ~$66.7M total impact)
- **A**: Contingency rates were set by third party; argue that PFS-level detail supports lower PM/monitoring percentages
- **R**: Directly reduces the $173M contingency component
- **T**: Submit revised ERC with negotiated rates by Q4 2027

---

### SMART-3: Eliminate House Demolition via Sale/Repurpose

- **S**: Sell 505 company houses to employees/community at nominal cost instead of demolishing
- **M**: Avoid **$11.8M demolition cost** (505 × $23,200); recover sale revenue
- **A**: Company towns have precedent for house sales (e.g., Queensland mining towns)
- **R**: Directly reduces Domain 9K ($18.2M); supports community transition strategy for Dustyroo Flats
- **T**: Implement house sale program 2027–2029 (before closure execution begins)

---

### SMART-4: Accelerate Progressive Rehabilitation

- **S**: Increase progressive rehab from $2.5M/yr to $5.0M/yr during remaining mine life (2027–2031)
- **M**: Reduce Domain 6 (Existing Rehab) from **$23.2M to ~$12M** (less area needing repair at closure)
- **A**: $2.5M additional/yr is within current operating budget capacity
- **R**: Reduces maintenance cost ($17.3M for 8,205 ha) and repair costs ($5.9M)
- **T**: 5-year program 2027–2031, revised Domain 6 estimate by 2031

---

### SMART-5: Reduce Holding Costs via Progressive Lease Relinquishment

- **S**: Relinquish rehabilitated portions of the lease progressively rather than waiting until 2050
- **M**: Council rates ($8.33M/yr) and land tax ($1.64M/yr) scale with lease area — relinquishing 50% by 2040 saves **~$50M** in holding costs
- **A**: QLD regulatory framework supports progressive relinquishment via PRCP (Progressive Rehabilitation and Closure Plan)
- **R**: Directly reduces the $233M holding cost component (48% of which is council rates)
- **T**: Submit first relinquishment application by 2035 (post-execution yr 4)

---

### SMART-6: Monetise Gas Drainage Post-Closure

- **S**: Continue gas drainage from 71 gas drainage boreholes (Domain 8C) post-closure for power generation/flaring
- **M**: Convert $14.1M borehole sealing cost into revenue — ACCU generation + gas sales
- **A**: Infrastructure exists; methane within gas contract specs
- **R**: Addresses carbon liability (20-year decommissioned mine emissions) + generates revenue
- **T**: System operational by Q2 2032 (6 months after cessation)

> **Context**: The $495M NPV saving vs the $389M Brave Blossom capital investment = **$106M net positive NPV** even before counting Brave Blossom revenue. This is a powerful argument for the go recommendation.
