## Recommended Top 3 (Draft)

### Recommendation 1: Progress Brave Blossom with Stage-Gated Funding
**Rationale**: Provides 20-year revenue extension, defers $900M closure liability, retains workforce, maintains community social license.

**Approach**:
- **Stage 1 (2027-2028)**: Fund $20M for PFS + exploration drilling ($10M studies + $10M drilling)
- **Stage Gate 1** (end 2028): PFS results, federal approval pathway clarity, updated resource estimate
- **Stage 2 (2029-2031)**: If PFS positive, fund development ($104M for shafts, drift, conveyors)
- **Stage Gate 2** (end 2030): Federal approvals secured, CHPP life extension committed, rail/port capacity confirmed
- **Stage 3 (2031-2033)**: Fund major equipment ($266M longwall, mining equipment, CHPP EPCM)
- **First Coal**: ~2033

**Key Risks**:
- Federal approval uncertainty (mitigate: early engagement with DCCEEW, parallel state/federal process)
- Coal quality deterioration (mitigate: further drilling, customer pre-commitment)
- Rail/port capacity (mitigate: secure 6mtpa allocation before 2028 contract renewal)
- CHPP end-of-life (mitigate: budget $24M EPCM in capital plan)

### Recommendation 2: Optimise Closure Liability with Progressive Strategy
**Rationale**: $900M is significantly higher than anticipated — active management can reduce this.

**Approach**:
- Challenge third-party estimate (engage independent review of QLD ERC tool inputs)
- Accelerate progressive rehabilitation during remaining mine life ($2.5M/annum → increase to $5M/annum)
- Repurpose pit voids (water storage revenue, tailings capacity, potential solar/renewables)
- Negotiate with third party for revised unit rates / scope optimization
- Target 15-20% reduction ($135M-$180M saving) via SMART measures

**SMART Target Example**:
- **S**: Engage independent quantity surveyor to review $900M estimate
- **M**: Target 15% reduction ($135M) in closure estimate
- **A**: Leverage current revenue stream to fund review
- **R**: Directly reduces post-mining liability
- **T**: Complete review by Q2 2028, revised estimate by Q4 2028

### Recommendation 3: Implement Carbon Abatement & Gas Monetisation
**Rationale**: Decommissioned mine emissions represent material liability over 20 years. Gas capture converts liability to revenue.

**Approach**:
- Extend gas offsite agreement (methane within contract specs) — negotiate extension clauses now
- Install post-closure gas capture infrastructure (drainage continuation + flaring)
- Generate ACCUs from methane capture/destruction
- Model Safeguard Mechanism baseline → plan abatement pathway
- Explore flooding sealed workings to suppress emissions
- Reforestation/vegetation on rehabilitated land for bio-sequestration

**SMART Target Example**:
- **S**: Install post-closure gas capture and flaring system at Springbok
- **M**: Reduce decommissioned mine emissions by 70% vs unmanaged baseline
- **A**: Gas drainage infrastructure already exists; extension is feasible
- **R**: Directly addresses carbon liability
- **T**: System operational by Q2 2032 (6 months after cessation)
