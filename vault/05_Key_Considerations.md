# 05 — Key Considerations

## CHPP (Coal Handling & Preparation Plant)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Design capacity | 8Mt/year feed |
| Current output | ~4mtpa saleable @ ~67% yield (Premium HCC) |
| Location | Close to current underground operations |
| Coal transport | Conveyed from underground to CHPP |
| Rejects | Hauled to nearby pit void |
| Tailings | Pumped to above-ground TSF adjacent to CHPP |
| End of life | 2032 (at current throughput) |
| Extension needs | New conveyor, raw coal bin overhauls, major structural repairs |

**Key question**: Can the CHPP support Brave Blossom's 8mtpa ROM? Major capital required for life extension.

## Carbon

### Regulatory Framework
- Carbon tax reforms expected to impact coal exports from **2027**
- Safeguard Mechanism: facilities emitting >100,000 tonnes CO₂-e annually are subject to regulation
- Emissions exceeding baseline → abatement or carbon credit purchase required
- Baseline levels outlined in valuation model

### Decommissioned Mine Emissions
- Defined under NGER Determination: coal production and active ventilation ceased, not expected to resume
- Emissions continue for **20 years** after cessation
- Material liability if no alternative carbon-reduction strategies implemented
- Gas assignment model encompasses both current operations and proposed project site

### Scope 1 Emission Sources
1. Fossil fuel combustion
2. Post-mining activities
3. Coal mine waste gas
4. Emissions from decommissioned mines
