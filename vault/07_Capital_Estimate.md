## Key Observations

1. **Front-loaded studies** (2027-2028): $20M for projects + exploration drilling — critical path for go/no-go
2. **Development window** (2029-2031): $104M for shafts, drift, conveyors — 3-year construction window
3. **Peak capital year 2032**: $221.5M — driven by $190M longwall purchase
4. **Longwall is the single largest item**: $190M (49% of total capex)
5. **CHPP life extension**: $24M EPCM in 2032-2033 (not included in the items above — overlaps with CHPP end-of-life at 2032)
6. **Timing constraint**: If work doesn't commence in 2027, cashflow gap after Springbok closure (2031) → loss of workforce, revenue gap
