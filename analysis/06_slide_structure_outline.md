## Design Principles

1. **Lead with the recommendation** (brief tip #1) — the board's answer is on Slide 2, not Slide 14
2. **Quantify everything** (tip #2) — every claim backed by NPV, $, %, or tCO₂e
3. **Address the no-go scenario** (tip #3) — explicitly evaluated, not glossed over
4. **Acknowledge risks honestly** (tip #4) — federal approval uncertainty front and centre
5. **Present a timeline** (tip #5) — stage gates and milestones visualised
6. **Board pitch, not technical report** (tip #7) — influence-driven, visual-first, minimal text
